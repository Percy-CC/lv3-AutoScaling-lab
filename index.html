模擬模型假設 Model assumptions

  • 1 tick = 1 分鐘 minute;每次模擬 240 分鐘(15:00–19:00)。Each run covers 240 minutes.
  • 規格 Specs:S = 100 rps ($1/min),M = 200 rps ($1.9/min),L = 400 rps ($3.6/min)。價格為虛構,並非阿里雲實際價格 Prices are fictional, not real Alibaba Cloud prices.
  • 新實例需 3 分鐘啟動(啟動期間已計費)。New instances take 3 min to boot and are billed meanwhile.
  • Load Balancer 約 2 分鐘後才發現故障實例。LB needs about 2 min to detect a failed instance.
  • Round Robin 平均分配流量;Least Connections 在此模型中近似按實例容量比例分配。Round Robin splits equally; Least Connections is approximated as capacity-proportional.
  • 可用度 Availability = 成功處理的請求 ÷ 總請求。

A. 垂直縮放 Vertical (Scaling Up)

只有 1 台實例,改變規格。One instance, change its spec.

B. 水平縮放 Horizontal (Scaling Out)

多台實例 + Load Balancer。Several instances behind a load balancer.

Mission 4 · AI 預測性伸縮 Predictive Scaling

Reactive(被動)伸縮要等 CPU 超標才加機,而新實例要 3 分鐘才可服務,所以流量急升時會有一段時間不足。Predictive(預測)伸縮用過去流量預測 5 分鐘後的流量,提前加機。
Reactive scaling waits for high CPU and new instances need 3 min to boot. Predictive scaling forecasts the load 5 min ahead and scales earlier.

預設的預測方法是移動平均(Moving Average),它只反映過去,所以會滯後。請修改 predict(),令它在 trend 情境表現更好;再於 viral(突發爆紅)情境觀察預測何時失準。
The default is a moving average, which lags behind. Edit predict() to do better on trend, then see where predictions fail on viral.

注意:無限迴圈會令瀏覽器分頁停頓,執行前請避免 while(true)。Avoid infinite loops; they will freeze the tab.

提示 Hint 1

用最近 6 筆數據的變化量計算每分鐘增長率 slope,再用「最新值 + slope × 5」預測 5 分鐘後。Use the change over the last 6 samples as slope; predict latest + slope × 5.

提示 Hint 2

數據有隨機波動。可先用最近 3 筆平均值作起點,再計算斜率。Data is noisy; smooth with a 3-sample average before computing the slope.
